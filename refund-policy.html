Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the rules governing order cancellation and eligible refunds on Nova Cart.

1. Purpose

Nova Cart aims to provide a transparent and fair cancellation and refund process while protecting customers and preventing fraud.

2. Customer Cancellation

A customer may request cancellation before the financing process or order is completed. Cancellation requests are subject to verification and the status of the application or order. Once financing has been approved or an order has progressed beyond certain stages, cancellation may not be available or may be subject to additional conditions, as permitted by applicable law and the financing agreement.

3. Company Cancellation

Nova Cart may cancel an application or order for valid reasons, including but not limited to:

  • Customer request
  • Failed identity verification
  • Incomplete or incorrect information
  • Incomplete documentation
  • Eligibility requirements not met
  • Payment verification issues
  • Duplicate applications
  • Fraud prevention checks
  • Security concerns
  • Violation of Nova Cart policies
  • Technical or operational issues
  • Any other lawful or reasonable ground

4. Fake Documents or False Information

  • Fake documents
  • Altered or forged documents
  • False or misleading information
  • Another person's identity
  • Fraudulent financial records

Nova Cart may reject the application, cancel the order, suspend or permanently block the account, restrict future applications, or take any other action permitted under applicable law.

5. Down Payment Refund

If an eligible order is cancelled before financing is completed, any eligible down payment received by Nova Cart will generally be refunded. Refund eligibility is determined after verification and in accordance with applicable law and Nova Cart's policies.

6. Refund Method

Approved refunds will generally be processed using the same original payment method through which the customer made the payment. Nova Cart does not ordinarily change the refund destination unless required by law or where technically necessary.

7. Refund Processing Time

Eligible refunds are normally initiated within 24 to 72 hours after approval of the cancellation. The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account depends on the relevant bank, UPI network, card issuer, or payment service provider. Nova Cart is not responsible for delays caused solely by third-party payment systems after the refund has been initiated.

8. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Fraudulent activity
  • Chargeback abuse
  • Identity misuse
  • Violation of Nova Cart policies
  • Misrepresentation by the customer
  • Other circumstances where refund is legally restricted

9. EMI Application Cancellation

Submitting an EMI application does not automatically create a financing agreement. Applications may be cancelled by either the customer (where permitted) or Nova Cart before final approval, subject to the stage of processing and applicable conditions.

10. Order Status

  • Pending
  • Under Review
  • Documents Required
  • Approved
  • Rejected
  • Cancelled
  • Refunded

These statuses are provided for informational purposes and may change as the application progresses.

11. Customer Responsibilities

  • Providing accurate information
  • Uploading genuine documents
  • Monitoring application updates
  • Responding promptly to verification requests

Failure to cooperate may result in delays, cancellation, or rejection.

12. Disputes

If a customer believes that a refund has not been processed correctly, they may contact Nova Cart using the official support email. Nova Cart will review the matter and respond within a reasonable period.

13. Policy Updates

Nova Cart reserves the right to modify this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. The updated version will become effective once published on the platform.

14. Contact
