Responsible Lending Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

Nova Cart is committed to responsible lending practices and fair treatment of customers.

1. Our Commitment

  • Fair and transparent financing
  • Responsible credit assessment
  • Customer protection
  • Ethical business practices
  • Secure verification processes
  • Respect for customer privacy

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.

2. Eligibility Assessment

  • Identity verification
  • Age eligibility
  • Residential information
  • Employment status
  • Monthly income
  • Existing financial obligations
  • Credit history (where available)
  • Document verification
  • Internal risk assessment
  • Fraud prevention checks

Nova Cart may request additional information whenever reasonably necessary.

3. Minimum Income

Applicants are generally expected to have a minimum monthly income of ₹20,000. Meeting the minimum income requirement alone does not guarantee approval.

4. Down Payment Policy

  • A minimum 15% Down Payment is generally required
  • Customers may voluntarily make a higher down payment
  • A higher down payment may reduce the financed amount and the corresponding EMI

5. Credit Assessment

  • Applicants with a good repayment history may receive preference
  • Applicants without a credit history may also be considered
  • Applicants with limited repayment issues may be reviewed individually

No approval decision is based solely on a single factor such as a credit score.

6. Employment Assessment

  • Salaried Employees
  • Self-Employed Individuals

Nova Cart may request supporting documents appropriate to the applicant's employment category.

7. Customer Verification

  • Aadhaar Verification
  • PAN Verification
  • Live Selfie Verification
  • Face Match Verification
  • Mobile OTP Verification
  • Live GPS Location (with customer consent)
  • Bank Statement Review (where required)
  • Salary Slip or Income Proof (where required)

8. Fair Decision Making

Each application is reviewed individually. Approval or rejection depends on the overall assessment and not on a single criterion. Nova Cart aims to make financing decisions fairly and consistently.

9. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information
  • Upload genuine documents
  • Cooperate during verification
  • Read all applicable terms before accepting financing
  • Make EMI payments on time

10. Financial Responsibility

Customers should carefully evaluate their financial circumstances before applying for EMI financing. Borrow only when you believe you can comfortably meet your repayment obligations.

11. Late or Missed Payments

Failure to make EMI payments on time may lead to actions permitted under the financing agreement and applicable law, including reminders, recovery processes, or other appropriate measures.

12. Fraud Prevention

Applications involving fake documents, false information, identity misuse, or other fraudulent activities may be rejected. Nova Cart may suspend or block accounts involved in confirmed policy violations or fraudulent conduct, subject to applicable law.

13. Transparency

  • Down Payment requirements
  • EMI tenure
  • Eligibility criteria
  • Verification requirements
  • Customer obligations
  • Refund and cancellation policies

Customers are encouraged to read all applicable policies before submitting an application.

14. Policy Updates

Nova Cart may update this Responsible Lending Policy from time to time to reflect changes in business practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. The latest version will be published on the platform.
