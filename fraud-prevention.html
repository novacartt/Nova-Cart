Fraud Prevention & Anti-Fraud Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

Nova Cart is committed to maintaining a secure financing platform and protecting customers from fraud and identity theft.

1. Purpose

Nova Cart is committed to maintaining a secure financing platform and protecting customers, business partners, and the Company from fraud, identity theft, financial abuse, and other unlawful activities.

2. Identity Verification

To support fraud prevention, Nova Cart may require customers to complete one or more verification steps, including:

  • Mobile Number Verification (OTP)
  • Identity Document Verification
  • Aadhaar & PAN Verification
  • Live Selfie Verification
  • Face Match Verification
  • Live GPS Location Verification (with customer's consent)
  • Additional verification where reasonably required

3. Document Verification

Customers must upload only genuine and valid documents. Applications may be declined if documents appear to be:

  • Fake
  • Forged
  • Altered
  • Expired
  • Incomplete
  • Misleading
  • Not belonging to the applicant

Nova Cart may request additional documents if verification cannot be completed.

4. False Information

  • Incorrect identity details
  • False employment information
  • Incorrect income information
  • Incorrect address details
  • False banking information
  • False references

Providing inaccurate information may result in rejection of the application or other actions permitted by law.

5. Multiple or Unauthorized Accounts

Customers must not create multiple accounts for fraudulent purposes or attempt to misuse another person's identity or account. Nova Cart may suspend or restrict accounts where fraudulent or unauthorized activity is reasonably suspected.

6. Fraud Monitoring

Nova Cart may monitor applications and account activity to help identify unusual, suspicious, or potentially fraudulent behavior. Where appropriate, additional verification may be requested before processing an application or providing services.

7. Consequences of Fraud

  • Reject the application
  • Cancel the order
  • Suspend or permanently block the account
  • Restrict future applications
  • Retain records as required by law
  • Cooperate with competent law enforcement or regulatory authorities where legally required

8. Customer Responsibilities

  • Providing accurate information
  • Protecting their account credentials
  • Reporting any suspected unauthorized access promptly
  • Cooperating with verification requests

9. Privacy

Verification and fraud prevention activities are conducted in accordance with Nova Cart's Privacy Policy and applicable laws. Information collected for fraud prevention is used only for legitimate business, security, and legal purposes.

10. Policy Updates

Nova Cart may revise this Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on the platform and will take effect from the date of publication.

11. Contact

For questions relating to fraud prevention or account security, please contact:
