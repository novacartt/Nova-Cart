Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Nova Cart uses cookies and similar technologies when you access or use our website, applications, and services.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve performance, remember user preferences, and enhance security. Nova Cart may also use similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage, and other technologies that perform similar functions.

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • Keep you securely signed in
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Improve website functionality
  • Enhance website performance
  • Provide a better user experience
  • Protect customer accounts from unauthorized access
  • Detect suspicious or fraudulent activity
  • Help diagnose technical issues
  • Measure website usage and improve our services

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the operation of the website and enable core features such as secure login, account authentication, session management, and navigation. Without these cookies, certain services may not function properly.

Security Cookies

  • Detect suspicious activity
  • Prevent unauthorized access
  • Protect customer accounts
  • Reduce fraud risks
  • Maintain platform security

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website so that we can improve speed, usability, and overall performance. Where required by law, these cookies will only be used with appropriate consent.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember certain user preferences, such as language or interface settings, to provide a more personalized experience.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Nova Cart may use trusted third-party service providers for functions such as payment processing, analytics, communication, or cloud hosting. These third parties may use their own cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy practices. Nova Cart does not control the cookie policies of third-party services.

5. Managing Cookies

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Configure cookie preferences

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality, security, or performance of the Nova Cart platform.

6. Data Collected Through Cookies

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Language preferences
  • Login session information
  • Website usage statistics
  • Security-related information

Nova Cart does not use cookies to intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, or banking credentials.

7. Cookie Retention

  • Session Cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies may remain until they expire or are deleted by you.

Retention periods may vary depending on the purpose of the cookie.

8. Updates to This Policy

Nova Cart may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices. The updated version will become effective once published on the platform.
