Consumer Rights Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

This Consumer Rights Policy explains the rights and responsibilities of customers using Nova Cart.

1. Our Commitment

  • Fair and transparent financing practices
  • Respecting customer privacy
  • Providing clear information about our services
  • Protecting customer data using reasonable security measures
  • Treating customers fairly throughout the application process

2. Right to Information

  • EMI products and services
  • Eligibility requirements
  • Down Payment requirements
  • Application process
  • Verification requirements
  • Refund and cancellation rules
  • Applicable policies and terms

Nova Cart aims to communicate this information in a transparent and understandable manner.

3. Right to Fair Treatment

Every customer is entitled to fair and respectful treatment. Applications are reviewed based on relevant eligibility criteria, verification results, and internal assessment processes. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.

4. Right to Privacy

Customer information is handled in accordance with Nova Cart's Privacy Policy and applicable laws. Nova Cart takes reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Right to Correct Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information and may request corrections to certain personal details where permitted and reasonably practicable. Providing false or misleading information may affect eligibility or result in other actions permitted by law.

6. Right to Know Application Status

  • Pending
  • Under Review
  • Documents Required
  • Approved
  • Rejected
  • Cancelled
  • Refunded (where applicable)

Nova Cart generally aims to communicate the final decision within 24 to 72 hours, although additional verification may require more time.

7. Right to Support

  • EMI applications
  • Verification
  • Refunds
  • Order cancellations
  • General platform support

Support Email: support.novacart@proton.me

8. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Submit genuine documents
  • Protect their account credentials
  • Cooperate during verification
  • Make EMI payments in accordance with the applicable financing agreement

9. Fraud Prevention

Nova Cart reserves the right to reject applications, cancel orders, suspend accounts, or take other actions permitted by applicable law where fraud, identity misuse, forged documents, or other serious policy violations are reasonably established.

10. Refund Rights

Where customers are eligible for a refund under Nova Cart's applicable policies, refunds are generally processed to the original payment method. Eligible refunds are normally initiated within 24 to 72 hours, although the actual credit timeline depends on the customer's bank or payment service provider.

11. Policy Changes

Nova Cart may revise this Consumer Rights Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on the platform and will become effective upon publication.

12. Governing Law

This Policy shall be governed by the laws of India. Subject to applicable law, disputes relating to this Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts located in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

13. Contact

For questions regarding this Consumer Rights Policy, please contact:

Nova Cart
Email: support.novacart@proton.me
