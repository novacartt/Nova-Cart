EMI Terms

EMI Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

These EMI Terms & Conditions govern the use of EMI financing services offered through Nova Cart.

1. EMI Financing

Nova Cart provides eligible customers with the opportunity to purchase eligible products through Equated Monthly Installments (EMI), subject to successful application review, verification, internal risk assessment, and approval. Submitting an application does not guarantee financing approval.

2. Eligible Products

EMI financing may be available on eligible products offered through the Nova Cart platform. Product eligibility may change without prior notice.

3. Minimum Down Payment

  • A minimum 15% Down Payment is generally required.
  • Customers may voluntarily pay more than the minimum down payment.
  • A higher down payment reduces the financed amount and may reduce the EMI payable.

4. EMI Tenure

  • 3 Months
  • 6 Months
  • 9 Months
  • 12 Months
  • 18 Months
  • 24 Months

Available tenure options may vary by product and eligibility.

5. Application Process

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Residential Address
  • Aadhaar Number
  • PAN Number
  • Employment Details
  • Income Details
  • Bank Details
  • Required Documents
  • Live Selfie
  • Reference Contacts

6. Eligibility Assessment

  • Identity Verification
  • Employment Verification
  • Income Assessment
  • Internal Credit Assessment
  • Risk Evaluation
  • Existing Financial Obligations
  • Document Verification
  • Fraud Prevention Checks

Meeting the basic eligibility criteria does not guarantee approval.

7. Credit History

  • Applicants with a strong repayment history may receive preference.
  • Applicants with no credit history may also be considered.
  • Applicants with limited or minor repayment issues may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

8. Document Verification

All documents submitted must be genuine, complete, and belong to the applicant. Submission of false, forged, altered, or misleading documents may result in rejection of the application and other actions permitted by applicable law.

9. Live Identity Verification

  • Live Selfie Verification
  • Face Match with submitted identity documents
  • OTP Verification
  • Device Verification
  • GPS Location Verification (with customer consent)

10. Application Review

After successful submission, applications generally undergo review. The final approval or rejection is normally communicated within 24 to 72 hours. If additional verification is required, the review period may be extended.

11. Order Confirmation

  • Successful verification
  • Approval of financing
  • Successful receipt of the required down payment (where applicable)
  • Completion of internal review

12. Order Cancellation

  • Failed verification
  • Incomplete documentation
  • Fraud detection
  • Eligibility requirements not met
  • Payment verification issues
  • Customer request
  • Violation of applicable policies

13. Refunds

If an eligible order is cancelled before financing is completed, any eligible refundable amount will generally be processed to the original payment method. Refunds are normally initiated within 24 to 72 hours.

14. Customer Responsibilities

  • Providing accurate information.
  • Maintaining updated contact details where required.
  • Making EMI payments on time.
  • Cooperating with verification requests.
  • Protecting account credentials.

15. Default

Failure to make scheduled EMI payments may result in actions permitted under the financing agreement and applicable law, which may include reminders, recovery processes, reporting where legally applicable, or suspension of services.

16. Prepayment

Where permitted under the applicable financing arrangement, customers may request early repayment or prepayment. Any applicable conditions or charges will be disclosed before processing such requests.
