Consent & eSign Policy

Effective Date: 06 August 2026

This policy explains how Nova Cart obtains customer consent and recognizes electronic actions during the EMI application process.

1. Purpose

This Policy explains how your electronic consent is collected and how your electronic acceptance of documents, agreements, and declarations is recorded during your use of Nova Cart.

2. Electronic Consent

By registering an account, submitting an EMI application, clicking "I Agree", "Accept", "Continue", "Submit", or any similar confirmation button, you provide your electronic consent to:

  • Submit your EMI application
  • Provide the information entered by you
  • Upload required documents
  • Complete the verification process
  • Receive electronic communications
  • Accept Nova Cart's applicable policies and agreements

3. Electronic Signature (eSign)

Where permitted by applicable law, your electronic acceptance through the Nova Cart platform may be treated as your electronic signature for applicable documents and agreements, including:

  • EMI Application Forms
  • Customer Declarations
  • Consent Forms
  • Terms & Conditions
  • Privacy Policy
  • Other applicable agreements

4. OTP Verification

Nova Cart may use One-Time Password (OTP) verification to confirm ownership of your registered mobile number before processing certain actions. An OTP is confidential and must not be shared with any other person.

5. Identity Verification Consent

By submitting your application, you consent to identity verification measures that may include:

  • Aadhaar Verification
  • PAN Verification
  • Live Selfie Verification
  • Face Match Verification
  • Document Verification
  • Mobile Verification

These checks are performed to help prevent fraud and verify your identity.

6. GPS Location Consent

Where location verification is required, Nova Cart will request your permission before accessing your device's GPS location. By granting permission, you consent to the collection and use of your live location for purposes such as:

  • Identity verification
  • Address verification
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Risk assessment

If you choose not to provide location access, some services or verification processes may not be available.

7. Customer Declaration

  • All information provided is true, accurate, and complete to the best of your knowledge
  • All uploaded documents belong to you
  • You have not knowingly submitted forged, altered, or fake documents
  • You understand that providing false information may result in rejection of your application, cancellation of your order, suspension of your account, or other actions permitted under applicable law

8. Communication Consent

  • SMS
  • WhatsApp
  • Email
  • In-app notifications
  • Telephone calls (where necessary)

These communications may include verification requests, application status updates, payment reminders, security alerts, and customer support messages.

9. Withdrawal of Consent

Where permitted by applicable law, you may withdraw certain consents by contacting Nova Cart. Please note that withdrawing consent may affect our ability to process your application or continue providing certain services.

10. Record Retention

Nova Cart may retain records of electronic consents, confirmations, and signatures for operational, legal, security, and audit purposes, subject to applicable laws.

11. Changes to this Policy

Nova Cart may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available on the platform and will become effective from the date of publication.

12. Contact
